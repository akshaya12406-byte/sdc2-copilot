### Video Structure & Script Summary

Below is a detailed breakdown of what is covered in the demonstration video based on the presentation script:

* **0:00 – 0:45 | Introduction & Business Problem**
  * Presented by **Team Tech Pros** from Prince Shri Venkateshwara Padmavathy Engineering College.
  * Highlights the core business problem: the repetitive hand-coding of Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2 (SCD2) pipelines.
  * Introduces the solution, **SCD2 Copilot**, which automates change detection, history tracking, data validation, and natural-language explanations.

* **0:45 – 2:40 | Core SCD2 Flow, Validation, & AI Explanations**
  * **Input Upload**: Demonstration of uploading today's source snapshot and yesterday's SCD2 table.
  * **Deterministic Logic**: Shows automatic key detection and classification (new, changed, unchanged, deleted) through visual examples (e.g., customer 101's city update closing the old version and inserting the new active version, and customer 104 being inserted as a new record).
  * **Automated Validation**: Runs checks for schema completeness, business key integrity, single active rows, overlapping date ranges, and date consistency.
  * **AI Insights**: Showcases the natural-language explanations generated by the LLM, alongside pipeline metrics (estimated cost, token usage, and API latency).

* **2:40 – 3:52 | Soft Delete Handling**
  * Demonstrates how the system handles records that exist in yesterday's database but are missing from today's source.
  * Shows how the pipeline marks the active row as closed (updating the effective end date) instead of executing a physical delete, ensuring a complete historical audit trail.

* **3:52 – 5:20 | Multi-Version History Tracking**
  * Demonstrates how the system maintains and tracks multiple historical versions of the same customer over time.
  * Explains how chronological history tracking enables compliance auditing, trend analysis, and historical reporting.

* **5:20 – 5:41 | Automated Testing & Conclusion**
  * Highlights the automated testing framework and adversarial datasets used to validate robust handling of duplicate keys, schema drift, null values, and unicode characters.
  * Wrap-up summarizing how the prototype satisfies all hackathon requirements with production-ready code.
